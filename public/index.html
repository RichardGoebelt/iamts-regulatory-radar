Connected & Automated Driving

How to use this Radar

This weekly radar helps IAMTS members identify regulatory and standardization developments in connected and automated driving that may affect Testing & Certification, future services and joint IAMTS activities.

1. Start with Priority & ChangeHigh flags the strongest Testing & Certification / urgency / IAMTS impact. New and Updated are compared with the previous successful monitoring cycle.
2. Filter and verifyUse Region, Status, Priority, Change and Search to focus the table. Open the linked official source before relying on an item for external decisions.
3. Turn signals into IAMTS actionUse Testing & Certification Relevance and IAMTS Questions / Implications to identify common methods, capabilities, engagement or collaboration opportunities.

Reading source status: “Successfully checked · 0 matching items” means the source was accessible but no relevant development matched the scope. “Currently unavailable” means the source could not be retrieved. For EU reference use, relevant EUR-Lex acts classified as In force are retained back to 1 January 2022; choose Region = EU and Status = In force to display them.
